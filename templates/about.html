OUR PURPOSE

Small actions. Meaningful impact.

Food Rescue is a simple platform concept that helps connect people with surplus food to people who may need it.

🌱

Reduce waste

Encourage people to share surplus food instead of throwing it away.

❤️

Support people

Make it easier for community members to discover food donations.

🌍

Build awareness

Promote responsible food use and community participation.

Important: This is an educational prototype. It does not verify food safety, donor identity, or pickup arrangements. Users must coordinate carefully and follow local food-safety rules.
